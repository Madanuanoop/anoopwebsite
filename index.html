Use these built-in checklists to organise your revision. Check official notifications independently before applying.

Universal exam preparation method

  1. List the syllabus and divide it into small topics.
  2. Study one concept and write a short summary in your own words.
  3. Practise questions without looking at the answers first.
  4. Keep an error notebook and revisit mistakes weekly.
  5. Take timed mock tests and review your performance calmly.

The Anoop Website

A student learning hub built to make studying easier through simple explanations, subject notes, coding examples, quizzes, and useful tools. This single-file version uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and its learning content is stored directly in the page.

Privacy note: Study notes, planner tasks, progress and preferences are stored in local browser storage on this device. They are not synced between devices. Avoid saving sensitive personal information.

Learning tip

Read a concept, explain it in your own words, practise an example, then revisit it after a day or two. For exams, confirm the latest syllabus and dates through official notices.
